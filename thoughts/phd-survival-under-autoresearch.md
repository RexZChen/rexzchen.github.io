lately, i was asked by friends and colleagues a few times about how phd students can survive under the current ai-does-all vibe, especially when autoresearch went viral.

so i translated the question to *can modern ai do solid research?*

and honestly, after using a bunch of these deep-thinking / research-oriented tools, i would say by far, we are safe.

here's why.

these tools are somewhat designed for problem solving from the top down. or in other words, i found their workflow is basically: (1) find surfaced evidence about explicit failures, and (2) aim for patches.

and that's fine for engineering. that's debugging. that's fixing things that are already broken in obvious ways.

but in a more researchy setup, the workflow is flipped. it's bottom up: (1) propose hypotheses about *potential* failures and verify them with surfacable evidence, and (2) transform those failures into design choices that mitigate the gaps.

see the difference? one reacts to what's broken. the other imagines what *could* be broken — and builds around it before it happens. that second thing is the actual hard part of research, and it requires a kind of taste and intuition that current tools just don't have.

like, ai can help you run experiments faster, write boilerplate, summarize papers, sure. but can it look at a system and go "hmm, i have a feeling this will fall apart under X condition, let me design around that"? not really. not yet. it doesn't have that gut feeling you build from reading 200 papers and failing 15 times.

so, if your research is mostly patching known issues, then maybe worry a little. but if you're doing the kind of work where you're asking new questions and designing from scratch? you're fine. the tools are assistants, not replacements.

the real skill isn't knowing the answer. it's knowing which question to ask. and that's still very much a human thing.

i always urge myself to 'look beyond appearances to grasp the essence' (透过现象看本质), which i dont see as a solid apperance in modern autoresearch.

at least for now lol.
