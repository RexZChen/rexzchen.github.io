MRI is a visual metaphor. Color represents tensor-derived computational statistics, not biological activity. The cube groups measurements; it is not the physical structure of a model.

Weights fixed. State evolving.

The cube is an abstract summary of model computation. Its axes group sequence positions, layers, and recorded components. Color and opacity represent the selected statistic.

Explicit reasoning is generated token computation. Those tokens become context for later output; they do not expose every internal computation. Direct models still compute through every layer.

Attention shows assigned attention mass, not automatically a causal explanation. Larger activation values do not imply greater causal importance or intelligence.

Hidden Δ is an approximate distance in a shared random projection when available; otherwise it is explicitly labeled a norm difference. Probability Δ is measured at the output, not at every layer.
