About Me

Hello! My name is Zirong (Rex) Chen (陈子荣), currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science at Vanderbilt University under Professor Meiyi Ma's supervision. My research interests include Natural Language Processing (NLP), Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS), and AI-driven applications for Smart Cities. I earned my bachelor's degree (B.E. in Computer Engineering) in China and my master's degree (M.S. in Computer Science) at Georgetown University. I am also a former basketball player in high school and college.

Research Projects

Logic-informed Call-taking Performance Debriefing

We decompose one-shot LLM runtime into modularized function calls enhanced with Signal Temporal Logic to evaluate call-taker's performance under real-world deployment.

Keywords: LLMs, NLP, AI for Social Goods

Customized Emergency Response Training

Collaborating with Metro Nashville Department of Emergency Communications, we develop Angie/SimER. SimER is an AI-driven simulation environment to assist call-taker training under emergency response scenarios.

Keywords: LLMs, NLP, AI for Social Goods

Learn More

Intelligent Emergency Response Systems

We created an intelligent system that leverages data to enhance response time and decision-making in emergencies. These systems use machine learning to improve coordination between protocols and streamline operations.

Keywords: AI/ML, NLP, AI for Social Goods

Learn More

Adaptable City Knowledge

The Adaptable City Knowledge project aims to create a dynamic, data-driven platform that helps cities respond to evolving challenges. From traffic management to disaster preparedness, this initiative fosters collaboration between city officials, businesses, and residents to promote resilience.

Keywords: AI/ML, NLP, Life-long Learning
